Core Pillars of the Mediterranean Diet

The Mediterranean diet is more than a short-term meal plan; it is a heart-healthy lifestyle centered around high-quality whole foods, fresh vegetables, healthy fats, and shared meals.

  • Healthy Fats: Extra-virgin olive oil is used as the primary cooking oil and salad dressing base.
  • Plant-Based Staples: Vegetables, legumes, nuts, seeds, and whole grains form the majority of every meal.
  • Moderate Seafood & Poultry: Fish rich in Omega-3 fatty acids (like salmon and sardines) are consumed at least twice a week.
  • Limited Processed Foods: Refined sugars, sodium, and heavily processed snacks are kept to a minimum.

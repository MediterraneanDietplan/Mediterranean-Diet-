Mediterranean Food Guide

Simple guidelines on what to focus on and what to reduce.

✅ Eat Frequently

  • 🥗 Vegetables: Tomatoes, spinach, kale, cucumbers, broccoli
  • 🥑 Healthy Fats: Extra virgin olive oil, avocados, olives
  • 🌰 Nuts & Seeds: Almonds, walnuts, chia seeds, sunflower seeds
  • 🐟 Seafood: Salmon, tuna, sardines, mackerel
  • 🌾 Whole Grains: Quinoa, brown rice, oats, whole wheat

❌ Limit or Avoid

  • 🥤 Added Sugars: Soda, candies, commercial sweets
  • 🍔 Refined Grains: White bread, refined pasta, white flour
  • 🥓 Trans Fats & Margarine: Processed oils and shortenings
  • 🌭 Processed Meats: Hot dogs, sausages, deli meats
  • 🥫 Ultra-Processed Foods: Meals with synthetic additives
